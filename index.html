What We Do

Founded in 2018, we provide consultancy services for the aerospace industry, with a specialisation in the defence sector. Our team of consultants have flown 4th and 5th generation combat air platforms; each with impressive backgrounds in strategy, capability development and operational delivery.

All of our consultants have combat experience and fast-jet instructional expertise. We currently work with UK, US, European and Middle Eastern clients utilising a network of specialists to provide tailored expertise and advice.

Operating Procedures

Development of robust operating procedures tailored to platform, mission and regulatory environment.

Specialist Advice

Direct access to specialist aerospace and tactical advice, drawn from operational fast-jet and test experience.

Programme & Advisory Support

Embedded support across acquisition, capability and programme advisory work.

Information Security

Every engagement is built around a premium on information security, from first contact through to delivery.

Why Us

Our team can enhance the output and performance of your organisation through timely, insightful and contemporary advice. We offer proactive and tailored expertise to clients, focussed on their requirements, always placing a premium on information security.

Our services span a broad remit of the aerospace sector to include the development of operating procedures, provision of specialist aerospace advice, creation of training and tactical syllabi and validation of best practice. We also work across the entire procurement chain with aerospace companies, third parties and end-users to develop and represent capabilities to ensure programmatic success.

Meet the Team

Since operations began in 2018, the LockOn team has grown from strength to strength. We are a committed and passionate team, who have continually delivered success on a diverse portfolio of projects.

We only recruit the very best. Our employees and associates are all drawn from the top tier of tactical, test and instructor pilots from cutting-edge 4th and 5th generation combat air platforms across the world.

Together with their considerable experience in strategy, capability development and operational planning, they possess unique skill sets that have proved highly valuable to our clients. If you are looking for a battle-proven team to make a positive difference to your organisation, please get in touch.
